Choose or drop the PDF of a standard. Clause Lab indexes clauses, definitions, annexes, tables, figures and “shall” requirements, then shows the document as one continuous, margin-trimmed scroll with the selected region framed in red.

Everything stays in this browser: the PDF is never uploaded. The index is cached locally and can be exported as JSON.
